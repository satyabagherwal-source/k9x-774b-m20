## 1. What AI-Builder-Brain Is (The Core Identity)

**AI-Builder-Brain is NOT an AI model, not a chatbot, not an autonomous agent, not an LLM, and not merely a code generator or knowledge repository.**

It is a **Universal, Model-Independent, Evidence-Based Construction Intelligence DATA SYSTEM**.

Its ultimate mission is to empower any compatible AI model (GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, local Ollama models, or future frontier systems) to:
- **Discover & Research** user needs, market dynamics, and domain science
- **Plan & Architect** sound, modular, scalable system specifications
- **Design** coherent user experiences, interfaces, tokens, and information architectures
- **Build & Assemble** production-ready source code, manifests, and configurations across any tech stack
- **Test & Verify** code behavior empirically via automated assertions and negative constraints
- **Deploy & Operate** resilient services with observability, security, and recovery
- **Learn & Evolve** continuously from real-world execution evidence and feedback loops

### The 4-Way Separation of Concerns

1. **AI Models (Cognition)**: Provide reasoning, understanding, strategic planning, decision making, and orchestration. AI models think; they do not serve as persistent storage.
2. **AI-Builder-Brain (Data & Intelligence)**: Stores and supplies structured, connected, recallable intelligence data, engineering patterns, governing rules, and verification evidence. The Brain never thinks on its own.
3. **Tools & Infrastructure (Execution)**: Compilers, package managers, test runners, Git, Docker, cloud APIs, and browsers execute concrete operational tasks.
4. **Products (Outcomes)**: Independent digital deliverables deployed to serve real end-users.
5. **Feedback & Evolution (Learning)**: Real test results, defect autopsies, and operational signals are harvested, validated through independent barriers, and promoted into the Brain to improve future construction.

## 7. Governing Invariants & Safety Laws

1. **Law of Addition & Enrichment**: Never delete, remove, or weaken existing files, folders, data, rules, or tests. Evolution is strictly additive.
2. **Dual-Track Git Policy**: 
   - Master Brain permits safe auto-commits with anti-spoofing and exclusion barriers (`autoPush: false`).
   - Child projects generated by Project Factory are permanently restricted to manual user commits (`CHILD_PROJECT = MANUAL_COMMIT_ONLY`).
3. **Production Cache Integrity**: The 384 MB retrieval cache (`.project-brain/cache/retrieval-index.json`) and 16 canonical directories must never be arbitrarily overwritten or destroyed.
4. **Empirical Truth Barrier**: Never claim a capability is `DONE` or `VERIFIED_WORKING` without concrete test execution, non-zero assertion counts, and exit code 0 proof.
5. **No Hallucinated Progress**: Once a task or capability is marked `DONE` with verified evidence, future agents must never repeat or restart that work.
