## 2. Forensic Discoveries & Root Causes

1. **Sitemap Contradiction**:
   - `scripts/generate-sitemap.mjs` dumped 39 localized utility routes (`/es/about/`, etc.) into `public/sitemap.xml`.
   - `public/robots.txt` pointed to `sitemap.xml`.
   - Meanwhile, Astro templates correctly flagged those routes with `noindex={true}`.
   - The project's existing audit script only checked `dist/sitemap-0.xml` generated by `@astrojs/sitemap`, creating a false-positive pass while Googlebot consumed `public/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Anchor Fragment in Redirection**:
   - `/frames/` redirected to `/generator/#decoratorSection 301`.
   - Fragment identifiers in 301 headers violate RFC 3986 and are flagged by GSC.
3. **Query Parameter Duplication**:
   - Header search button navigated via `/?search=true#fontSearchInput`, creating duplicate crawl targets canonicalizing to `/`.
4. **Thin Category Pages**:
   - 9 category pages had only ~190 words of boilerplate catalog text, causing Google's quality algorithms to de-index them after crawling.
5. **Crawl Budget Exhaustion**:
   - 616 URLs were simultaneously submitted on a new domain without priority or changefreq metadata.

---

## 3. The Core Meta-Learning: Why Rules Did Not Prevent the Failure

1. **Temporal Asynchrony**: Rules 1489–1491 were harvested from `onlinefreeprotractor` on **October 17, 2026**, but `old-english-font-free` was built on **October 12, 2026**.
2. **Retrieval Failure**: The workspace lacked `.project-brain/` mapping, so older SEO rules were not automatically loaded during previous sessions.
3. **Scope Drift Invalidation**: A previous 53-phase quality release certificate audited only 45 English routes and was never re-run when 587 localized routes were added in commit `9204246`.
