## 7. Failures, Anomalies & Resolved Gaps

1. **Monolithic Destination Defaulting in Legacy Router:**  
   *Discovery:* In `destination-router.mjs`, 696 of 697 candidates defaulted to `05_KNOWLEDGE` because `knowledgeType` is missing in candidate JSON records.  
   *Resolution:* Implemented role-aware type inference in `IsolatedDistributionEngine` that parses title and structural intent to distribute knowledge across `03_SKILLS`, `08_VERIFICATION`, `11_INBOX`, `12_DECISIONS`, `14_EVOLUTION`, and `05_KNOWLEDGE`.

2. **Keyword Deficit for Machine Learning Terminology:**  
   *Discovery:* 226 candidate records were unclassified because ML terms (`eos`, `pooling`, `encoder`, `sparsity`) were missing from `INTELLIGENCE_DOMAINS` keywords.  
   *Resolution:* Expanded heuristic keyword vectors for Domain H (Technical Construction) to include modern compilation and inference tokens, eliminating the classification blindspot.

3. **Frontmatter Omission in Markdown Serialization:**  
   *Discovery:* Raw title/content file writes omitted metadata tags, causing BM25 tag-based retrieval to miss items whose content body used synonyms.  
   *Resolution:* Implemented structured YAML frontmatter serialization in `persistAndReadBack()`, ensuring tags, domains, and sources are indexed and retrievable.
